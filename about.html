About Rwanda Health

Rwanda's healthcare system has made remarkable progress since the 1994 genocide, transforming from one of the weakest in Africa to a model for development. The country now boasts universal health coverage through community-based health insurance schemes.

🏥 Healthcare Infrastructure

Rwanda has over 500 health facilities across 30 districts, including 4 referral hospitals, 35 district hospitals, and numerous health centers and clinics.

👥 Community Health Workers

Over 45,000 community health workers provide essential services at the grassroots level, ensuring healthcare reaches every village.

📈 Key Achievements

  • Life expectancy increased from 49 to 69 years
  • Maternal mortality reduced by 77%
  • Under-5 mortality decreased by 70%
  • 95% of population covered by health insurance

🚨 Emergency Services

24/7 ambulance services available nationwide. Call 912 for immediate emergency response across all provinces.
