Great question! Here are some common challenges Rwandans face that websites/digital platforms could help solve:
Agriculture (biggest sector)

Farmers lack access to real-time market prices, so middlemen exploit them
Hard to find buyers for produce directly
Limited access to weather forecasts and farming advice

Healthcare

Difficulty finding nearby health centers or checking doctor availability
Long queues at hospitals — online appointment booking would help
Lack of accessible health information in Kinyarwanda

Education

Limited access to quality learning materials, especially outside Kigali
Students struggle to find tutors or extra academic help
Exam preparation resources are scattered or hard to find

Jobs & Business

No centralized job board focused on the Rwandan market
Small businesses (especially in villages) have no online presence to reach customers
Freelancers have nowhere local to advertise their skills

Government & Services

People travel far just to access information about public services
Lack of awareness about government programs (loans, grants, Irembo services)
Land and legal information is hard to navigate

Real Estate & Rentals

Finding affordable housing is largely word-of-mouth
No reliable platform to compare house/land prices across districts

Transport

No reliable way to check bus schedules or book intercity travel
Moto-taxi pricing is inconsistent and non-transparent
