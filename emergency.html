Emergency Services

When every second counts, NearCare helps you access life-saving care immediately.

🚨 Immediate Action

If you're in immediate danger, call 912 now for ambulance dispatch. Our system locates the nearest emergency facility and guides responders to your location.

🏥 Emergency Facilities

Rwanda has dedicated emergency units in major hospitals across all provinces. Use our locator to find the closest one and get directions.

Emergency Preparedness Tips

  • Know your location and nearest health facility
  • Keep emergency contacts saved in your phone
  • Have health insurance information ready
  • Learn basic first aid for common emergencies
